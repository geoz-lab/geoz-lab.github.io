About

I'm a PhD candidate in Energy Resources Engineering at Stanford University (Frank G. Miller Fellow), passionate about tech and sustainability. I specialize in omni-, vision-, and large / small language models, deep learning, physical modeling, numerical simulation, and experimental prototyping — pairing fine-tuning (SFT, DPO, PPO, GRPO, RLHF), RAG, and agents with reinforcement learning and generative vision.

I've shipped ML systems at TikTok, Valuenex, Chevron, and Fervo Energy, and authored 15+ papers (150+ citations) across AI and computational science. I care about the full arc — from a research idea to a model running reliably at scale — and about translating between researchers, engineers, and stakeholders to get there.

Energy & Sustainability

My work sits at the center of the energy transition — pairing machine learning with subsurface science to make carbon storage, geothermal energy, and hydrogen storage safer, cheaper, and easier to scale. Across a Stanford PhD and industry roles at Chevron and Fervo Energy, I've built models that turn messy reservoir and market data into decisions that cut cost and de-risk deployment — including full-chain carbon-capture analysis with the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative that surfaced $2M+ in potential savings.
